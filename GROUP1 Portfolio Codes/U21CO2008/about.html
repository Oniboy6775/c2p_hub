About Me

A closer look

Who I am

I'm Oyeyemi Joshua Oluwagbotemi, a Embedded System Engineer and tech & innovation enthusiast currently completing my final year (500 Level) at Ahmadu Bello University, Zaria. My interest lies at the intersection of control systems and intelligent automation, designing systems that can sense a problem and act on it without constant human input.

That interest has shaped the two projects I'm most proud of so far: an autonomous drone that detects and treats crop disease, and a home smoke-detection and alarm system. Both reflect the same underlying goal, using engineering to solve everyday problems before they escalate.

How I think

I approach problems the way a control engineer is trained to: identify the signal that matters, understand the feedback loop, and design a system that responds reliably. I bring the same mindset to learning new tools; MATLAB for simulation, Python for logic and data, and HTML/CSS for building the interfaces that make a system usable.

Outside of coursework, I stay curious about where robotics, agriculture, and safety technology intersect, and I'm always looking for the next small system worth building.
