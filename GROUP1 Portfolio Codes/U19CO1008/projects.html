COMPUTATIONAL INTELLIGENCE

Pipeline Leak Detection System

Led a team building a leak-detection system entirely from scratch in MATLAB — no toolboxes. A 3→10→6→3 feedforward neural network (Xavier init, ReLU, softmax, cross-entropy) reached ~97.6% accuracy on backpropagation alone, improving to ~99.8% once GA and PSO optimizers took over the weight search. Paired with a hand-built Mamdani fuzzy logic system and a real-time IoT dashboard in MATLAB App Designer.
