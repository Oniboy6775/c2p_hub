Execution Flow & Professional Journey

01. THE FOUNDATION

My path started with hands-on hardware — wiring microcontrollers, laying out PCBs in KiCad, and learning how a signal on a breadboard turns into working firmware. That bottom-up habit still shapes how I work: understand the stack from silicon to software before trusting an abstraction.

That same first-principles approach carried over into machine learning — building neural networks and control systems from scratch in MATLAB and C/C++ rather than reaching for a library, so the math behind every layer is something I actually understand.

Core Variables & What Drives Me

01: FIRST PRINCIPLES

I'd rather build the matrix math, the activation function, or the driver myself than import it. Understanding a system fully, not just using it is how I trust what I ship.

02: HARDWARE + AI

Most people specialize in one side of the stack. I work across both — PCB design and embedded firmware on one hand, from-scratch ML pipelines on the other — because the most interesting problems live at that intersection.

03: BUILD TO LEARN

Coursework, competitions, and independent projects are all the same exercise to me: pick something I don't fully understand yet, and build it until I do.

04: SHIP REAL THINGS

Alongside academics, I'm building a hardware startup — because a design only proves itself once it's a working board in your hand, not a diagram on a slide.
