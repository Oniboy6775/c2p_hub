A little about her

Khadija spends her days moving between two worlds: research-driven product design and fast, honest content creation. One informs the other — the same curiosity that shapes a user flow shapes a script.

She's spent the last few years designing for early-stage products, running usability sessions, and building the kind of interfaces that don't need a tutorial. Alongside that, she's grown an audience by documenting the design process itself — the messy parts included.

When she's not in Figma or in front of a camera, she's usually scouting a new coffee spot or reorganizing her moodboards for the third time this week.
