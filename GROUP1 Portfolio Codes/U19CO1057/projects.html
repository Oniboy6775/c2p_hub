Autonomous Drone for Crop Disease Detection & Control

Final Year Project, Ahmadu Bello University, Zaria; 2024/2025

  • Designed and built a fully autonomous F450 drone with a Pixhawk flight controller capable of executing GPS waypoint missions.
  • Developed a real-time crop disease detection model using YOLOv8n, deployed on a Raspberry Pi 4B as the onboard computer.
  • Integrated computer vision with autonomous flight control so the drone can identify and respond to diseased crop zones without manual input.
  • Managed full hardware-software integration: embedded Linux setup, model optimisation for edge inference, and MAVLink communication.
