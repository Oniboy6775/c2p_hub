Personal Introduction

My academic and practical journey has immersed me in the principles of computer networks, systems programming, IoT architectures, cybersecurity defenses, and telecommunications infrastructure.

Academic & Professional Identity

My core foundation is in Computer Engineering with a deliberate specialization in the Networking Track. I am deeply captivated by the intersection of hardware and softwarehow low-level communication protocols, embedded controllers, and distributed cloud services interact to build resilient, secure, and scalable solutions for real-world challenges.

Background & Industry Exposure

Throughout my undergraduate program, I have cultivated hands-on competencies in network configuration, embedded IoT systems development, Python scripting, and web technologies. Crucially, I gained direct industry experience during my internship with MTN Nigeria Communications Plc in the Network Access Planning and Optimization (NAPO) unit. There, I engaged with live cellular networks (2G, 3G, 4G, and 5G), participated in RF drive tests using TEMS Investigation, analyzed network KPIs using MAPS, and performed geographic base station site planning using Google Earth Pro and MapInfo GIS.
