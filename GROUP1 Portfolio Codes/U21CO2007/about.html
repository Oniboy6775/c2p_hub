My Story

My name is Abdullahi Onisabi, a Computer Engineering student at Ahmadu Bello University, Zaria, expecting to graduate in 2026. Alongside my formal studies, I taught myself full-stack web and mobile development, focusing on the MERN stack (MongoDB, Express.js, React, and Node.js) and React Native.

I like building things that solve real problems my most notable project so far, DataReloaded, is a live VTU platform where users can buy data, airtime, cable TV subscriptions, and pay electricity bills online.

I believe consistency beats talent when talent doesn't work hard, and I try to grow a little every day whether that's learning a new tool, refining an old project, or reading about emerging technology.
