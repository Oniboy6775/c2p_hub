Get Started

Engineering the Future of Innovation.

C2P Tech Hub is a forward-thinking technology and innovation center established to empower individuals, startups, and communities with digital skills, entrepreneurial support, and access to emerging technologies. Located in the Department of Computer Engineering, ABU Zaria, we serve as a catalyst for inclusive innovation and sustainable development.

Our Mission

To build a vibrant ecosystem that fosters innovation and empowers entrepreneurs and learners with digital skills, and supports the creation of scalable technology-driven solutions for social and economic development.

Our Vision

To become Africa's leading community-driven tech innovation hub that transforms ideas into scalable tech solutions and connects local talents to global opportunities.

Our Story

From a Study Group to a Structured Hub

C2P Tech Hub began as an informal study circle among Computer Engineering students determined to close the gap between coursework and the tools employers actually expect. What started as weekend coding sessions has grown into a structured hub with dedicated training halls, a C2P Lab, and a track record of student-led ventures moving from prototype to market.
