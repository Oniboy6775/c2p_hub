About Me

My name is Olorundare Olorunfemi Emmanuel, and I'm from Iffe town, Ijumu Local Government Area, Kogi State, Nigeria. I got into networking out of pure curiosity I just wanted to know how networking devices actually work, how one computer "talks" to another across a network. That curiosity pushed me to start Cisco classes, and I surprised myself with how fast I was catching on.

The moment it really clicked for me was during my SIWES industrial training, when I got to configure an access point myself for the first time. It sounds small, but watching a device actually connect and work because of settings I typed in was one of the most satisfying things I'd experienced. I didn't get it right on the first try I spent a good while confused about why devices weren't connecting the way I expected, until I figured out what I'd missed. That struggle taught me more than any lecture could have.

Outside of networking, I'm a pretty normal guy I've probably rewatched some of my favourite movies more times than I've reconfigured a router, and I always have music playing in the background while I work. My goal is to become a network engineer who can set up and secure networks for companies, and someday train other young people who are curious about tech the way I was.
